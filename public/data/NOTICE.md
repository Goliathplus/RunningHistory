# Data notices

- `polities.json` is derived from **Cliopatria v0.2.1** (Seshat Global History Databank), CC BY 4.0
  <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. Bennett, J. S. et al. (2025), *Scientific Data* 12, 247,
  <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9>.
  Changes: relation records removed, geometry simplified (~3 km), attributes re-encoded, six year-0 endpoints
  adjusted, Chinese names added.
- Chinese names: Wikidata (CC0), reviewed and partly corrected by hand.
- `battles.json`: dates, coordinates and labels from Wikidata (CC0); selection, belligerents, results, war groupings and
  Chinese names curated for this project.
- `land-50m.json`: Natural Earth (public domain), packaged by `world-atlas` (ISC).
